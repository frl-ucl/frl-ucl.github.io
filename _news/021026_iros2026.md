We are pleased to share that the FRL Group participated in **IROS 2026**, held from **September 27 – October 1** in **Pittsburgh, USA**, one of the world's leading conferences in robotics and intelligent systems.

Our group contributed to the conference through workshop organisation and research presentations, highlighting our ongoing work in autonomous and marine robotics.

:small_blue_diamond: **Workshop Organisation – AMBRE**
**Sara Aldhaheri** co-organised the workshop *"AMBRE: Adaptive Manipulation – Bridging Rigid and Soft Robotics in Underwater Environments"*, held on October 1. The workshop brought together researchers working in underwater robotics, manipulation, and soft robotics to explore new approaches to adaptive interaction and autonomous operation in challenging underwater environments.

:small_blue_diamond: **Research Presentation**
**Song Ma** presented his work, *"Active Tracking of Marine Pollution Sources: An Uncertainty-Aware Categorical Bayesian Framework for Unmanned Surface Vehicles"*, showcasing research on uncertainty-aware decision-making and active tracking strategies for marine pollution monitoring using autonomous surface vehicles.

We are proud to see our team actively contributing to IROS 2026 through both workshop organisation and research presentations. These contributions reflect FRL's continued commitment to advancing autonomous systems, marine robotics, and intelligent technologies for ocean applications.
