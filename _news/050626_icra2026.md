We are pleased to share that the FRL Group participated in **ICRA 2026**, held from **June 1–5** in **Vienna, Austria**, one of the world’s leading conferences in robotics and automation.

Our group contributed to the conference through research presentations and workshop participation, showcasing our ongoing work in autonomous and marine robotics.

:small_blue_diamond: **Main Conference Paper**

**Yang Hu** and **Sara Aldhaheri** presented their research, sharing new advances in robotics and contributing to discussions with the international robotics community.

:small_blue_diamond: **Xplore Workshop**

**Ye Li** presented two works at the [**Xplore Workshop**](https://xplore-workshop.github.io/), focusing on variable-resolution virtual maps and informed planning for autonomous unmanned surface vehicles (USVs):

- *Variable-Resolution Virtual Map Guided Informed Tree Search for Autonomous USV*
- *Variable-Resolution Virtual Maps for Autonomous Exploration with Unmanned Surface Vehicles*

These contributions highlight the group's ongoing research into efficient exploration, uncertainty-aware mapping, and autonomous navigation for marine robotic systems.

We are proud to see our team actively contributing to ICRA 2026 and engaging with researchers from around the world. These presentations reflect FRL's continued commitment to advancing robotics, autonomy, and marine technologies.
