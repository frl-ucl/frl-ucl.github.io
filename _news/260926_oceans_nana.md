We are pleased to share that **Nana Kutin** presented his research at **OCEANS 2026**, held in **Monterrey, USA**.

:small_blue_diamond: **Research Presentation**
Nana presented his work, *"Deep Reinforcement Learning for Optimal Weather Routing of Wind-Assisted Ships"*, exploring how deep reinforcement learning can optimise ship routing to minimise fuel consumption while accounting for changing ocean and weather conditions.

The study demonstrates how intelligent routing can complement wind-assisted propulsion, achieving fuel savings of **8.2%** compared with a distance-based route and highlighting the potential of AI-driven approaches for more efficient and sustainable maritime operations.

We are proud to see FRL research contributing to advances in maritime autonomy and decarbonisation at OCEANS 2026.
