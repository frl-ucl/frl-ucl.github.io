We are pleased to share that the FRL Group participated in **AUV 2026**, held in **Southampton, UK**, bringing together researchers, industry professionals, and practitioners working in autonomous underwater systems and marine robotics.

Our group contributed to the event through research presentation and panel participation, highlighting our ongoing work in underwater autonomy, intelligent monitoring, and autonomous marine systems.

:small_blue_diamond: **Research Presentation**
**Nana Kutin** presented his work, *"Spatio-Temporal Graph Neural Network for Autonomous Anomaly Detection in Underwater Glider Telemetry"*. The research explores the application of spatio-temporal graph neural networks to detect anomalies in underwater glider telemetry data, supporting more intelligent monitoring of autonomous underwater platforms and improving the ability to identify unusual system behaviour during long-duration ocean missions.

:small_blue_diamond: **Guest Panel – Marine Autonomy**
**Sara Aldhaheri** joined a guest panel discussion titled *"Marine Autonomy's Journey – Overlooked or Overhyped Through Different Lenses?"*. The discussion brought together different perspectives on the development, adoption, and future of marine autonomy, exploring its current impact, challenges, and opportunities across research and industry.

We are proud to see FRL members contributing to AUV 2026 through both technical research and wider discussions on the future of marine autonomy. These contributions reflect our group's continued commitment to advancing intelligent autonomous systems and enabling technologies for underwater and marine applications.
